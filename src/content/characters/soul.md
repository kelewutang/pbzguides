## Quick answer

Soul is the central playable character in *Phantom Blade Zero*. Official material identifies him as an elite assassin serving an organization called The Order, and frames his story around a remaining lifespan of 66 days. His journey follows an effort to uncover the truth behind what happened to him before that time runs out.

This is pre-release information. S-GAME has established Soul's role and the game's central premise, but has not published a complete biography, a final account of every relationship, or the full outcome of his story.

## Who is Soul?

S-GAME's official material places Soul at the center of *Phantom Blade Zero*. The player embodies him in the game's current official setup, making him both the protagonist and the central playable character.

The published description identifies Soul as an elite assassin. It also frames the game in the Phantom World, a fictional setting that blends elements of traditional Chinese martial arts, fantasy, science fiction, and folk horror. That establishes the world and Soul's role within it without providing an official full biography, age, birthplace, family history, or personal relationships.

## What is The Order?

The official PlayStation Blog introduction describes The Order as an elusive but powerful organization served by Soul. It is the confirmed organization most directly connected to his starting situation in the published story setup.

The reviewed official material does not publish The Order's hierarchy, leaders, factions, political purpose, or full history. This page therefore uses the organization only in the limited context S-GAME has made public: Soul serves it, and it is central to the premise around him.

## Why does Soul have 66 days?

Official descriptions give Soul a limited remaining lifespan of 66 days. The S-GAME announcement presents him as a hero whose life is rapidly coming to an end, while the PlayStation Blog says his temporary cure lasts for 66 days.

The story follows Soul as he attempts to uncover the mastermind behind the events surrounding him before his time runs out. Official material also says he was framed for the murder of The Order's patriarch and gravely injured in the ensuing manhunt. These are the public narrative premises, not a complete explanation of the condition itself.

The reviewed sources do not establish whether the 66 days are literal gameplay days, a visible in-game timer, a mechanic that can be extended or reset, or a condition with a fully disclosed cause. They also do not disclose the final outcome of Soul's story.

## Is Soul the playable character?

Yes. The official announcement says that players embody Soul, and the PlayStation Blog describes *Phantom Blade Zero* as a game in which the player is Soul. These sources support describing him as the central playable protagonist.

They do not publish a complete list of playable characters or rules for character switching. This page does not infer alternate protagonists, co-op, party members, or multiplayer modes from Soul's central role.

## What is confirmed about Soul's combat role?

Soul is the player character within the game's confirmed weapon framework. Official gameplay material says that, in battle, players can equip two Main Weapons and two Phantom Edges; it describes the main arsenal as including melee and ranged weapons, while Phantom Edges provide less conventional approaches.

That supports a high-level connection between Soul and the game's weapon-based combat choices. It does not publish Soul's full move set, skill tree, statistics, level system, talent progression, or final combat balance. For the system's confirmed loadout, upgrades, accessories, and difficulty information, see the [Phantom Blade Zero Combat System overview](/combat/combat-system/).

## What is not confirmed yet?

Official pre-release material does not yet provide a complete breakdown of Soul's skills, stats, character progression, relationships, or story outcomes. It also does not confirm a full backstory, secret identity, alternate forms, voice casting, relationship system, multiple endings, or what happens when the 66-day premise reaches its conclusion.

Those details should remain open until S-GAME publishes them. The current official material is enough to establish Soul's protagonist role, connection to The Order, and the central 66-day premise without filling the gaps with speculation.
