## Quick answer

Mó Yuan is **[Soul's father](/characters/soul/)** in *Phantom Blade Zero* and the **Leader of the Bastion of Mó**. S-GAME identifies him as the man shown cradling an infant in the pre-order trailer. Donnie Yen provides Mó Yuan's facial and motion capture, and also serves as a Creative Consultant on the game.

These are confirmed pre-release facts verified on September 8, 2026. Official material establishes Mó Yuan's connection to Soul, his leadership title, and Donnie Yen's production role, but it does not publish a complete biography, account of his motivations, or final story outcome.

## Donnie Yen's role

Official posts credit Donnie Yen as Mó Yuan's **Facial & Motion Capture Actor**. S-GAME's pre-order announcement says Mó Yuan is portrayed through facial and motion capture by Donnie Yen, while PlayStation's State of Play post says Yen brings the character to life through that performance work.

Yen also serves as a **Creative Consultant** on *Phantom Blade Zero*. S-GAME and PlayStation describe his martial-arts and action-filmmaking expertise as a contribution to the game's broader kung fu style and combat presentation. This supports crediting his performance and creative guidance without claiming that he voices Mó Yuan, wrote the character, designed every animation, or personally choreographed every move.
