## Quick answer

Seven Stars is an officially revealed boss encounter in *Phantom Blade Zero*. S-GAME's Year of the Snake gameplay trailer presents an unedited, complete combat sequence against Seven Stars: seven swordsmen fight as a coordinated formation before a second phase called **String Puppet** changes the encounter substantially.

This page records what S-GAME has revealed in pre-release footage and description. It is not a launch-version strategy guide. The trailer establishes the encounter's public presentation, but it does not establish final retail balance, rewards, or a verified way to defeat it.

## What is Seven Stars?

S-GAME calls the showcased opponent **Seven Stars** and presents the sequence as an epic boss fight. The official Year of the Snake trailer is a complete, unedited combat sequence rather than an isolated montage, so it is useful evidence of the encounter S-GAME chose to reveal before release.

The available official description identifies seven swordsmen and their coordinated fighting concept. It does not name the individual swordsmen, explain their wider faction or history, place the encounter in a particular location or story chapter, or say whether it is required, optional, repeatable, or unchanged for launch. Those details remain unconfirmed.

## Seven Stars Sword Formation

S-GAME names the initial coordinated concept the **Seven Stars Sword Formation**. Its description says that seven swordsmen move as one, alternating between coordinated group assaults and individual strikes. This establishes the formation as a group encounter with changing attack presentation, rather than a profile of seven separately identified characters.

The official article also says that their tactics adapt in real time. That is S-GAME's description of the revealed combat sequence, not a technical specification for artificial intelligence, procedural behavior, dynamic difficulty, or a guaranteed unique attack order on every future attempt. The public source does not explain the system behind that wording, so this page does not infer one.

## String Puppet second phase

S-GAME explicitly introduces **String Puppet** as the second phase of the Seven Stars encounter. Its description says that surviving disciples manipulate their leader like a marionette, suspending him on crimson threads. The leader sways erratically in mid-air, and the official text says his attack patterns become more frantic and harder to anticipate.

These are the confirmed features of the showcased second phase. The source does not publish a phase-transition health threshold, identify which disciples survive, explain whether the threads can be broken, name weak points, or define stagger and counter rules. String Puppet is therefore described here as the official second phase, not as a separately confirmed boss.
