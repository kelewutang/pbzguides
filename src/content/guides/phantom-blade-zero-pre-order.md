## Quick answer

*Phantom Blade Zero* pre-orders are officially open. The current S-GAME pre-order announcement names **PlayStation 5**, **Steam**, and the **Epic Games Store**. The official edition presentation lists two pre-order early unlocks: the **Treasure Basin** accessory and the **Legacy** outfit. S-GAME and PlayStation both say that those two items can also be obtained through normal in-game progression.

This is current official purchase information verified on September 8, 2026. Storefront availability, edition presentation, and other purchase details can change, so check the relevant official store page before placing an order. This page does not publish prices, regional availability, preload details, or an early-access period because the reviewed sources do not establish them.

## What are the Phantom Blade Zero pre-order bonuses?

The official English site identifies two items as pre-order bonuses:

- **Early Unlock Accessory: Treasure Basin**
- **Early Unlock Outfit: Legacy**

“Early Unlock” is the important part of the published wording. The official site says the content can also be obtained through in-game progression, and the PlayStation announcement specifically confirms that both Treasure Basin and Legacy are obtainable that way. They should therefore not be described as permanently exclusive pre-order items.

The current official material does not publish Treasure Basin's effects, statistics, or exact progression requirement. It also does not provide a detailed description of Legacy beyond its official outfit name. PBZ Guides keeps the bonus information at that verified level instead of assigning game effects or cosmetic details that have not been published.

## Where can you pre-order?

S-GAME's current pre-order announcement says pre-orders are open on **PS5**, **Steam**, and the **Epic Games Store**. These labels describe two different things:

- **PlayStation 5** is a hardware platform and its PlayStation Store listing is the relevant storefront.
- **PC** is the platform for the Steam and Epic Games Store listings; Steam and Epic are PC storefronts, not separate hardware platforms.

That is the current announced pre-order coverage. The reviewed announcement does not include Xbox or Nintendo storefronts, but an omission from the current list is not proof that a future version can never be announced. For the platform question on its own, see [Phantom Blade Zero Platforms](/guides/phantom-blade-zero-platforms/).

## Which edition should you pre-order?

S-GAME's official website currently presents a Standard Edition and a Deluxe Edition. Both include the full game and the two listed early unlocks. The Deluxe Edition adds two outfits labeled Deluxe Edition Exclusive, plus a digital artbook and digital soundtrack.

The useful choice is therefore about the listed contents, rather than a universal value judgment. Players who want the full game and the current early unlocks can compare the Standard Edition; players who specifically want the listed Deluxe cosmetic and digital extras can compare the Deluxe Edition. Prices, regional price differences, upgrade paths, and future sales are not established by the reviewed official sources. For the complete current contents comparison, see [Phantom Blade Zero Editions](/guides/phantom-blade-zero-editions/).

## Is pre-order required for the bonuses?

No, not if the question is whether Treasure Basin and Legacy can ever be obtained otherwise. Official information describes them as early unlocks and says they can also be obtained through in-game progression. Pre-ordering is the currently described way to receive them early; it is not presented as the only possible way to obtain them.

The sources do not state when the progression unlock happens, what requirement grants either item, or whether the process is identical across every storefront. Those details should not be inferred from the phrase “Early Unlock.”
