## Quick answer

S-GAME's official English site currently presents two *Phantom Blade Zero* editions: **Standard Edition** and **Deluxe Edition**. Both include the Full Game. Pre-ordering either edition grants early unlock of the **Treasure Basin** accessory and **Legacy** outfit; those items can also be obtained through in-game progression. The Deluxe Edition additionally lists **Wulin Legend**, **Blood-Steel Form**, a **Digital Artbook**, and a **Digital Soundtrack (OST)**.

This comparison reflects official information verified on October 12, 2026. It separates edition contents from conditional pre-order benefits. The reviewed sources do not establish a globally applicable price, an upgrade path, physical editions, a collector's edition, a season pass, or early access, so none are assumed here.

## Standard Edition contents

The Standard Edition includes:

- **Full Game**

If pre-ordered, it also grants early unlock of **Treasure Basin** and **Legacy**. The official site says both can also be obtained through in-game progression. The [PlayStation game page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/phantom-blade-zero/) specifies Treasure Basin's 10% Remnant Dust drop-rate increase, but the reviewed sources do not state exact progression timing or unlock requirements.

## Deluxe Edition contents

The Deluxe Edition currently lists:

- **Full Game**
- **Deluxe Edition Exclusive Outfit: Wulin Legend**
- **Deluxe Edition Exclusive Outfit: Blood-Steel Form**
- **Digital Artbook**
- **Digital Soundtrack (OST)**

Pre-ordering the Deluxe Edition also grants the same Treasure Basin and Legacy early unlocks. They are pre-order benefits, rather than content that distinguishes Deluxe from Standard.

The official English page explicitly labels Wulin Legend and Blood-Steel Form as “Deluxe Edition Exclusive Outfit.” That label supports describing them as the listed Deluxe-only outfits in the current presentation. It does not establish that they can never be offered through another method, on another platform, in a future bundle, or as a separate purchase. No such longer-term availability claim is made here.

## What is the difference between Standard and Deluxe?

The listed difference is straightforward: both editions include the Full Game, and pre-orders of either receive the same two early unlocks. The Deluxe Edition adds the two specifically labeled outfits, the digital artbook, and the digital soundtrack. The official content list does not identify earlier access to the game, extra story content, post-launch DLC, or a season pass.

That distinction matters when comparing editions. The early unlocks should not be treated as a reason to assume an exclusive gameplay item, because S-GAME says they can also be earned through progression. The two Deluxe outfits and the digital extras are the additional current listed contents.
