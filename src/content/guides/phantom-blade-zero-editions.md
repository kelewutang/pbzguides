## Quick answer

S-GAME's official English site currently presents two *Phantom Blade Zero* editions: **Standard Edition** and **Deluxe Edition**. Both list the Full Game, the early-unlock Treasure Basin accessory, and the early-unlock Legacy outfit. The Deluxe Edition additionally lists **Wulin Legend**, **Blood-Steel Form**, a **Digital Artbook**, and a **Digital Soundtrack (OST)**.

This comparison reflects official information verified on September 8, 2026. It compares listed contents only. The reviewed sources do not publish stable global pricing, regional prices, an upgrade path, physical editions, a collector's edition, a season pass, or early access, so none are assumed here.

## Standard Edition contents

The Standard Edition currently lists:

- **Full Game**
- **Early Unlock Accessory: Treasure Basin**
- **Early Unlock Outfit: Legacy**

The official site says that the early-unlock content can also be obtained through in-game progression. This means Treasure Basin and Legacy are not described as permanently exclusive to a pre-order. The available official material does not state their exact progression timing, unlock requirements, game effects, or item statistics.

## Deluxe Edition contents

The Deluxe Edition currently lists:

- **Full Game**
- **Early Unlock Accessory: Treasure Basin**
- **Early Unlock Outfit: Legacy**
- **Deluxe Edition Exclusive Outfit: Wulin Legend**
- **Deluxe Edition Exclusive Outfit: Blood-Steel Form**
- **Digital Artbook**
- **Digital Soundtrack (OST)**

The official English page explicitly labels Wulin Legend and Blood-Steel Form as “Deluxe Edition Exclusive Outfit.” That label supports describing them as the listed Deluxe-only outfits in the current presentation. It does not establish that they can never be offered through another method, on another platform, in a future bundle, or as a separate purchase. No such longer-term availability claim is made here.

## What is the difference between Standard and Deluxe?

The listed difference is straightforward: both editions include the Full Game and the same two pre-order early unlocks, while the Deluxe Edition adds the two specifically labeled outfits, the digital artbook, and the digital soundtrack. The official content list does not identify a gameplay advantage, earlier access to the game, extra story content, post-launch DLC, or a season pass.

That distinction matters when comparing editions. The early unlocks should not be treated as a reason to assume an exclusive gameplay item, because S-GAME says they can also be earned through progression. The two Deluxe outfits and the digital extras are the additional current listed contents.
