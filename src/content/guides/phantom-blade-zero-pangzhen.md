## Quick answer

S-GAME describes **Pangzhen Township** as a never-before-seen location in *Phantom Blade Zero*. Its 2025 RTX reveal connects the location to the earlier *Rainblood* town that Soul entered at the beginning of the IP, and says the new version uses high-precision 3D models based on scans of multiple ancient Chinese towns.

The same official reveal describes Pangzhen as a richly detailed, perilous key location that is perpetually rain-drenched. It is a setting reveal and graphics showcase, not a map guide: the reviewed material does not establish Pangzhen's size, route layout, quests, characters, enemies, bosses, rewards, or how and when players enter it.

## What is Pangzhen?

S-GAME's English announcement calls the current *Phantom Blade Zero* location **Pangzhen Township**. It introduces it as a location not previously shown for the game. The announcement also uses **Pang Town** when referring to the earlier hand-drawn map, so this page treats those names as the current location and its stated predecessor context rather than as separate destinations.

Pangzhen is confirmed as a key location with a dangerous, rain-drenched atmosphere. The announcement does not make it a complete regional map or confirm how it fits into the game's wider geography. For the broader setting terms S-GAME has published, see the [Phantom Blade Zero World and Setting](/guides/phantom-blade-zero-world/).

## Pangzhen and Rainblood

S-GAME says that, fifteen years earlier, its predecessor *Rainblood* depicted a town shrouded in conspiracy. According to the studio, Soul entering that town began an unforgettable wuxia tale and marked the origin of the entire IP.

That establishes a direct historical connection between Pangzhen's reveal and *Rainblood*. It does not establish that *Phantom Blade Zero* recreates every scene, route, or event from the earlier game. Nor does it reveal what role the location has in the new game's complete plot. For the separately published official premise of Soul's current journey, see [Phantom Blade Zero Story: Official Premise](/guides/phantom-blade-zero-story/).

## How Pangzhen was rebuilt

S-GAME says the former hand-drawn map of Pang Town has been transformed through high-precision 3D models based on scans of multiple ancient Chinese towns. That is the announced basis for the location's modern visual reconstruction.

The reveal describes a richly detailed, perilous, perpetually rain-drenched key location. It does not identify every scanned town, publish the playable map's boundaries, or confirm that the final release will match the showcase scene-for-scene. The rain-drenched description is also an announced atmosphere, not confirmation of a dynamic-weather system.

## RTX and DLSS 4 showcase

S-GAME says *Phantom Blade Zero* will launch with ray tracing and DLSS 4 with Multi Frame Generation, and presents Pangzhen Township with RTX ON. NVIDIA's Gamescom 2025 technical article adds that the PC presentation uses ray-traced reflections, shadows, and caustics, and says its accompanying video shows much of that technology in action in Pang Town.

Those statements describe a graphics showcase and the announced PC technologies. They do not establish performance on a particular PC, a universal visual setting, or identical final-release presentation. S-GAME's later [official PC system requirements](/guides/phantom-blade-zero-system-requirements/) publish separate ray-tracing and path-tracing targets for the game as a whole. That later path-tracing target must not be read back into the 2025 Pangzhen showcase, which the reviewed announcement describes as ray tracing and DLSS 4 with Multi Frame Generation.

## What is still unknown?

The reviewed official material does not publish Pangzhen's map size, boundaries, open-world status, routes, fast travel, chapter placement, entry conditions, missions, NPCs, merchants, bosses, enemy distribution, rewards, or collectible locations. It also does not establish a dynamic-weather system, a complete exploration loop, or a one-to-one visual match between the reveal and the final release.

Until S-GAME publishes those details, Pangzhen is best understood as an officially revealed location with a documented *Rainblood* connection and a specific RTX presentation, rather than as a walkthrough-ready area.
