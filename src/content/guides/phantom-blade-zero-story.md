## Quick answer

*Phantom Blade Zero* follows Soul, an elite assassin who serves an elusive and powerful organization called The Order. In the officially revealed premise, Soul is framed for the murder of The Order's patriarch and is gravely injured during the manhunt that follows. A mystic healer saves him from death, but the makeshift cure lasts only 66 days. Soul must uncover the mastermind behind what happened before his time runs out.

This is an official pre-release premise overview, not a complete plot summary. S-GAME has revealed the setup for Soul's journey, but it has not published the game's full story, the mastermind's identity, or its final outcome.

## What is Phantom Blade Zero about?

The current official setup places players in the role of Soul. The story begins with an accusation: Soul is framed for the murder of The Order's patriarch. The resulting manhunt leaves him gravely injured, and a mystic healer's treatment keeps him alive only temporarily.

That treatment gives the premise its urgency. Official material says the makeshift cure lasts 66 days, while Soul tries to find the mastermind behind the events that set the story in motion. The source presents that as the reason he must act before his time runs out; it does not disclose the person responsible, the full conspiracy, or every event that follows.

The game is set in the wider [Phantom Blade Zero world and setting](/guides/phantom-blade-zero-world/), where S-GAME has described a dark wuxia environment alongside Chinese Kungfu, unusual machinery, occult arts, and other elements. Those setting details give the premise context without establishing a complete history of every place or group Soul encounters.

## Who is Soul?

Soul is the player character in the official premise and an elite assassin serving The Order. He is the central figure in the story setup, but this page keeps the focus on the opening situation rather than treating it as a complete character biography.

For Soul's confirmed role, 66-day condition, and the character-specific details that remain unpublished, see the [Soul character profile](/characters/soul/). The official sources reviewed here do not establish his full life story, all relationships, abilities, or eventual fate.

## What is The Order?

The Order is the elusive and powerful organization that Soul serves in the official premise. The same public description identifies the murder of The Order's patriarch as the accusation against him.

That is the current confirmed scope. S-GAME has not published an organizational chart, membership list, headquarters, internal factions, political role, complete history, or a simple moral classification for The Order. This page therefore uses the name only where it is needed to explain Soul's setup, rather than presenting it as a fully documented faction.

## Why does Soul have 66 days?

According to the official description, a mystic healer saves Soul from death after he is gravely injured, but the healer's makeshift cure lasts only 66 days. This gives the revealed story premise a time-pressure element: Soul needs to uncover the mastermind before the cure runs out.

The source does not say that this is a literal gameplay timer, a real-time countdown, a quest-failure rule, or a system that can be extended or reset. It also does not explain the exact cause or full mechanics of the condition. Those are story and gameplay details that remain unpublished.

The game also has a separately named difficulty option called [Sixty-Six Days](/combat/difficulty-modes/). The reviewed sources do not establish a mechanical connection between that difficulty name and Soul's narrative 66-day condition, so they should be understood as separate published references.

Official material says Soul must find out the mastermind behind the events surrounding the accusation and manhunt before his time runs out. That establishes the central mystery of the premise.
