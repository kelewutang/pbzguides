## Does Phantom Blade Zero Support Controllers?

Yes. The [official Steam store listing](https://store.steampowered.com/app/4115450/Phantom_Blade_Zero/) currently marks *Phantom Blade Zero* as having **Full Controller Support** and says that the developer recommends playing with a controller.

That storefront label describes the current PC listing. It does not establish keyboard-and-mouse quality, full remapping behavior, support for every controller model, or feature parity with the PS5 version. For confirmed PC availability and storefront context, see [Phantom Blade Zero Platforms](/guides/phantom-blade-zero-platforms/). For the published hardware targets, see [Phantom Blade Zero System Requirements](/guides/phantom-blade-zero-system-requirements/).

## PC Controller Support on Steam

Steam currently lists **Full Controller Support** for the PC version. Its controller section names Xbox Controllers, PlayStation Controllers, and the Steam Input API, and separately states that the developer recommends playing with a controller.

These are the specific current Steam listing details. The listing does not provide a complete input map, remapping description, controller-by-controller compatibility list, or a statement about third-party controllers, so those details remain unconfirmed here.

## Xbox and PlayStation Controller Support

Steam's current controller section explicitly lists **Xbox Controllers** and **PlayStation Controllers**. This supports the storefront-level statement that those controller categories are listed for the PC version.

It does not identify every supported device, connection method, layout, or feature. In particular, Steam listing PlayStation Controllers for PC does not show that PC has the same DualSense adaptive-trigger or haptic-feedback features described for PS5.

## DualSense Features on PS5

For PS5, the [official PlayStation Store listing](https://store.playstation.com/en-us/product/UB2134-PPSA34579_00-0679049422108833) says that vibration function and trigger effect are supported with the DualSense wireless controller.

The [official PlayStation game page](https://www.playstation.com/en-us/games/phantom-blade-zero/) also describes adaptive triggers and haptic feedback for the PS5 version. It connects the adaptive triggers to the resistance of actions such as drawing a bowstring or charging a cannon, and haptic feedback to combat impacts such as blade clashes and hammer strikes.

Those are PS5-specific official feature descriptions. They are not evidence that the PC version supports DualSense adaptive triggers, haptic feedback, or identical controller behavior.

## Steam Deck Status

As checked on September 12, 2026, Steam shows *Phantom Blade Zero*'s **Steam Deck Compatibility** as **Unknown**. This is a point-in-time storefront status, not a performance result or a compatibility verdict.

An Unknown label must not be read as unsupported, incompatible, playable, or verified. Steam's current listing does not provide an official Steam Deck frame-rate target, resolution target, settings recommendation, or a future compatibility rating. This page should be reviewed again if Steam changes that status or publishes a rating.

## What Is Still Unknown Before Launch

The reviewed official sources do not establish PC DualSense feature parity, Steam Deck performance, a Steam Deck Verified or Playable rating, full controller remapping details, compatibility with all third-party controllers, or a comparison between controller and keyboard-and-mouse play.
