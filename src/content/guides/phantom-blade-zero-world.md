## Quick answer

S-GAME's official material calls **Phantom World** the universe in which *Phantom Blade Zero* is set. It describes that setting as a meeting point for Chinese Kungfu, intricate machinery with steampunk-like qualities, occult arts, and other unusual elements. The developer calls the overall art direction **Kungfupunk**.

Later official material describes the **Wulin** as the game's dark wuxia world: a martial world of conflict and intrigue where Soul encounters citizens, hidden secrets, and some side quests. The sources use both terms, but they do not establish an exact geographic hierarchy between them. This page explains the published setting without turning it into a complete map or lore database.

## What is Phantom World?

The 2023 official description calls Phantom World the universe in which the game is set. It presents a setting where different kinds of powers converge: Chinese Kungfu, intricate machines reminiscent of steampunk, occult arts, and elements that do not fit neatly into those categories.

This is a broad setting description, not a technical inventory of every magic, machine, faction, or culture in the game. The sources reviewed for this page do not establish a complete history, a political map, country names, borders, or a full geographic structure for Phantom World.

## What does Kungfupunk mean?

Kungfupunk is S-GAME's own name for the game's overall art direction. The studio describes it as adding a heavy dose of punk spirit to the formula alongside stylish visuals, drawing on a mix that includes wuxia influences and unusual fantasy and technological elements.

The term is useful for describing the presentation S-GAME has announced. It is not confirmed as an in-world faction, a technology category, a combat system, or a label with a detailed ruleset. For the announced equipment and combat framework, see the [Phantom Blade Zero Weapons hub](/weapons/) and the [Combat System overview](/combat/combat-system/).

## What is the Wulin?

In the 2026 State of Play discussion, S-GAME calls the Wulin its dark wuxia world. It describes a place marked by conflict and intrigue, where Soul encounters citizens in need during his journey. The same discussion says secrets are hidden throughout the Wulin as Soul learns the truth behind the events that begin the story.

The source also calls it a perilous martial world. That supports using Wulin as the current official term for the setting Soul explores in this material. It does not establish that Wulin is a continent, country, all of Phantom World, or a defined subregion within it. The relationship between the two terms remains undescribed in the reviewed sources.

## Is Phantom Blade Zero open world?

S-GAME describes *Phantom Blade Zero* as a **semi-open world**. In the 2023 official material, the developer contrasts that approach with one huge continuous open world. Instead, it describes multiple maps of reasonable size that are intended to be handcrafted and populated with diverse activities.

This establishes the studio's stated high-level world structure. It does not confirm an exact map count, map sizes, seamless loading, fast travel, zone order, region accessibility, a checklist system, or a particular exploration genre. “Semi-open world” is the official description, while the exact practical shape of exploration remains to be shown.

## Side quests and exploration

The State of Play material says Soul encounters citizens in need, and that interacting with them and completing **some** side quests might open new story paths and deepen a player's understanding of the Wulin. The qualifier matters: the source presents these as possibilities, not a complete account of every quest.

This does not confirm branching endings, permanent campaign states, morality systems, faction reputation, romance choices, or a full quest structure. It supports a narrower conclusion: official material connects some citizen interactions and side quests with story paths and learning more about the martial world.

Exploration also has a narrative role in the published description. S-GAME says secrets are hidden throughout the Wulin while Soul learns the truth behind the events that set the story in motion. For the official setup of that mystery, see the [Phantom Blade Zero Story: Official Premise](/guides/phantom-blade-zero-story/).

## What locations are known?

The reviewed sources establish broad setting language, but they do not provide a complete location database or map. They do not identify every region, settlement, route, dungeon, capital, or activity sequence.

One separately revealed location is [Pangzhen in *Phantom Blade Zero*](/guides/phantom-blade-zero-pangzhen/). S-GAME's Pangzhen announcement supports its relationship to *Rainblood* and its visual reconstruction, but it does not establish the town's geographic relationship to Phantom World or the Wulin.

## What is still unknown?

The available official material does not publish the full map, precise geography, exact number of maps, their sizes, travel rules, or the order in which players visit them. It also does not explain all groups, governments, political conflicts, side-quest outcomes, or the complete history of Phantom World and the Wulin.

That leaves the setting intentionally incomplete before release. The current sources are enough to establish the developer's terminology, atmosphere, high-level exploration model, and some story-facing interactions without inventing a world taxonomy that S-GAME has not announced.
