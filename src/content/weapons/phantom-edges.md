## Quick answer

Phantom Edges are *Phantom Blade Zero*'s secondary weapon category. S-GAME's official material says Soul can equip two Phantom Edges alongside two main weapons in battle, and currently identifies **25 secondary weapons known as Phantom Edges**. They are presented as unusual weapons that expand combat expression beyond the main arsenal.

The game is still pre-release. Official sources have described the category and its role, but they have not published a complete list of all 25 Phantom Edges or a full set of rules for each one.

## What are Phantom Edges?

S-GAME describes Phantom Edges as unique weapons scattered throughout the world of *Phantom Blade Zero*. In the current official framing, they are secondary weapons rather than the game's main weapon category.

The studio contrasts them with the main arsenal's melee and ranged options. Phantom Edges are described as less conventional in approach, supporting more intricate and devastating attacks and giving players greater room for combat expression. That identifies their place in the weapon system, but it does not establish a shared resource system, cooldown, input, damage rule, or activation rule for every Phantom Edge.

## How many Phantom Edges are there?

S-GAME currently says there are **25 secondary weapons known as Phantom Edges**. This is a confirmed count, not a complete public catalogue. The reviewed official sources do not name or describe all 25 individually, so this page does not treat the announced number as proof that every Phantom Edge has been revealed.

## How many Phantom Edges can you equip?

Official 2024 gameplay material says that, in battle, Soul can equip **two main weapons and two Phantom Edges**. The sources reviewed here do not explain every rule for changing that loadout, including whether equipment can be swapped during combat, so no such rule is assumed.

## How are Phantom Edges different from main weapons?

Main weapons are the primary arsenal. S-GAME has described that arsenal as including a wide selection of melee and ranged weapons, and later official material says the game has more than 30 main weapons.

Phantom Edges are the secondary category: the current official description presents them as more unconventional options that complement the main arsenal. This distinction does not mean Phantom Edges are consumables, spells, skills, passive perks, or ultimate abilities. It also does not establish universal rules about their damage, charges, energy cost, or cooldowns.

## Can Phantom Edges be upgraded?

In its 2026 State of Play deep dive, S-GAME says weapons can be upgraded and paired with accessories to shape a build. It also says an upgraded weapon can be reforged so its materials are recovered and reinvested in another weapon.

That is official information about the broader weapon system. The reviewed source does not publish a complete Phantom Edge-specific upgrade and reforging rule set, so this page does not claim that every Phantom Edge definitely follows the same upgrade path or can be reforged individually.

## What can Phantom Edges do?

The official discussion around the weapon system says players have options for fighting from range and using summons to assist in battle. Those are examples of combat possibilities discussed alongside the broader arsenal; they are not proof that every Phantom Edge is ranged, that every Phantom Edge can summon assistance, or that either feature belongs only to Phantom Edges.

The more specific official description remains the safest one: Phantom Edges are secondary weapons with less conventional approaches to combat. Additional mechanical detail needs a source that identifies a particular Phantom Edge and its finalized behavior.

## How do you unlock Phantom Edges?

S-GAME says unique Phantom Edges are scattered throughout the world. Its State of Play deep dive also says defeating some enemies expands the player's arsenal. Together, those statements support exploration and some enemy encounters as ways the arsenal can grow, but they do not provide a complete acquisition rule for all 25 Phantom Edges.

No official full acquisition list, exact location list, or boss-drop guarantee appears in the reviewed sources. This page therefore does not infer one.

## Are all 25 Phantom Edges known?

No complete official list is published in the reviewed sources. The count of 25 is confirmed, but individual names, statistics, rankings, rarity, upgrade caps, and best combinations remain outside the public information used here.
