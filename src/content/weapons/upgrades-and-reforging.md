## Quick answer

S-GAME has confirmed that weapons in *Phantom Blade Zero* can be upgraded. If a player reconsiders an upgraded weapon or wants to change a build, the studio says it can be reforged: its materials are returned so they can be reinvested in another weapon.

This confirms an upgrade and material-recovery framework. It does not yet publish the names of materials, upgrade levels, costs, stat effects, refund amount, or the precise rules for each weapon category. Reforging is not described as a global character respec.

## Can you upgrade weapons in Phantom Blade Zero?

Yes. The 2026 State of Play deep dive says players can upgrade their weapons. The statement is broad enough to confirm that weapon progression is part of the published system, but it is not a complete upgrade guide.

The reviewed sources do not name a crafting bench, menu, NPC, currency, material type, upgrade tier, maximum level, or stat tree. They also do not show how an upgrade is obtained or whether it changes damage, scaling, moves, effects, or another property. Those details should remain unpublished facts rather than assumed systems.

## What does reforging do?

S-GAME says that if players second-guess a weapon they have upgraded or want to switch up their build, they can reforge it, get those materials back, and reinvest them in another weapon. The confirmed purpose is to move upgrade materials from a reconsidered weapon into another weapon choice.

The source does not state whether every material is recovered, what percentage is returned, whether a fee or limit applies, or whether reforging changes the weapon itself. It also does not define a cooldown, location, or requirement. The safest reading is the studio's stated material-reinvestment function, without turning it into a universal refund formula.

## Is reforging a respec system?

The published wording concerns an **upgraded weapon** and returning its materials for reinvestment in another weapon. It does not describe resetting character levels, attributes, skills, quest decisions, or a whole account. For that reason, it should not be called a global respec system.

It is also not confirmed that every Main Weapon and every Phantom Edge follows identical upgrade or reforge rules. The official description addresses the broader weapon system; category-specific conditions need their own published evidence.

## Main Weapons, Phantom Edges, and upgrades

The current [Weapons hub](/weapons/) explains the broad system that S-GAME has revealed: more than 30 Main Weapons, 25 secondary weapons called Phantom Edges, and varied combat options. Those counts provide context for why material reinvestment may matter, but they do not reveal every individual weapon or upgrade path.

The battle loadout shown in S-GAME's official gameplay material includes two Main Weapons and two [Phantom Edges](/weapons/phantom-edges/). That establishes separate weapon categories and equipped slots. It does not prove that every member of either category uses the same materials, has the same cap, or can be reforged under identical conditions.

## Accessories and builds

S-GAME says weapons can be paired with different accessories to create the build a player wants. In the same official discussion, reforging is presented as an option for someone who wants to change that build after upgrading a weapon.

This supports a high-level connection between weapons, accessories, and build choices. The source does not publish accessory-slot counts, bonuses, set effects, combinations, or recommended builds. It also does not establish that a particular accessory changes an upgrade result. Current coverage should therefore describe build shaping, not prescribe an optimal setup.

## How do you get more weapons?

The State of Play deep dive says defeating some enemies expands the arsenal. It also says that players will unlock more than 30 Main Weapons and 25 Phantom Edges while exploring the Wulin. These statements support an expanding arsenal, but they do not create a complete acquisition table.

No reviewed official source establishes a universal boss-drop rule, a shop, a crafting system, fixed locations, random drops, or a full enemy-reward list. The fact that some enemies expand the arsenal should not be extended to every enemy or weapon.

## What is still unknown?

Important upgrade details remain unpublished: material names and quantities, upgrade caps, costs, stat changes, category-specific rules, reforge availability, material-recovery percentage, and any limits or penalties. The official sources also do not provide a complete weapon inventory, a recommended upgrade order, or evidence for the best build.

Those omissions matter because the game is still pre-release. This page records what S-GAME has announced, leaving later guides to add mechanical advice only when official or verified launch information supports it.

## FAQ

### Can weapons be upgraded?

Yes. S-GAME says weapons can be upgraded, but it has not published their materials, caps, or stat effects.

### Can you get upgrade materials back?

S-GAME says an upgraded weapon can be reforged so its materials can be returned and reinvested in another weapon. The exact recovery amount is not published.

### Does reforging reset your whole character?

No such global reset is confirmed. The official description concerns materials from an upgraded weapon.

### Can every Phantom Edge be reforged?

The broader weapon system includes upgrades and reforging, but the reviewed sources do not confirm an identical rule for every Phantom Edge or Main Weapon.
