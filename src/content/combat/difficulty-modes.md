## Quick answer

S-GAME has officially named three *Phantom Blade Zero* difficulty options: **Wayfarer**, **Hellwalker**, and **Sixty-Six Days**. Wayfarer is for players who would simply like to enjoy the story. Hellwalker raises the challenge with more advanced and adaptive enemy intelligence. Sixty-Six Days is presented as another demanding option that is not for the faint of heart.

Those descriptions establish the intended audience for each named option, but they are not a full mechanical specification. The official material reviewed for this page does not publish numerical damage or health changes, a complete ranked list of difficulties, or the rules for changing a selection during play.

## What difficulty modes are confirmed?

The August 2026 State of Play deep dive says the game's range of difficulty options is intended to let new players and experienced action-game players tailor the experience to their liking. It then names Wayfarer, Hellwalker, and Sixty-Six Days.

This is a confirmed set of named options in the reviewed source, not proof of an exhaustive launch menu. S-GAME may publish further settings or explain their order later. Until that happens, this page uses the names and descriptions that the studio has actually provided.

## Wayfarer mode

Wayfarer is the option S-GAME identifies for players who would simply like to enjoy the story. That gives it a clear accessibility and audience position within the published difficulty discussion.

The source does not call Wayfarer “Story Mode,” and it does not define its combat values. It does not say how enemy health, incoming damage, reaction behavior, checkpoints, assist prompts, rewards, or trophies would differ on Wayfarer. Calling it a story-focused choice is supported by the official description; assigning a numerical modifier or a specific assist system is not.

## Hellwalker mode

Hellwalker is the named option for players who want to increase the challenge. S-GAME specifically associates it with **more advanced and adaptive enemy intelligence**.

That is the published description of the challenge, but it does not explain the underlying AI systems. The source does not identify reactions, move selection, aggressiveness, recovery behavior, parry timing, damage scaling, or any other measurable rule. It would also be premature to assume that every Hellwalker change comes only from enemy intelligence rather than from other unpublished settings.

## Sixty-Six Days mode

S-GAME describes **Sixty-Six Days** as another challenging difficulty option and says it is not for the faint of heart. The wording confirms a demanding mode, without publishing its exact rules or placing it in a complete numerical hierarchy.

The difficulty-mode name should not be conflated with Soul's separate 66-day narrative premise. The [Soul character profile](/characters/soul/) records that premise only as published story context. The current official material does not establish a connection between the difficulty option and that narrative condition, so this page does not assign one.

## Can you change difficulty in Phantom Blade Zero?

The reviewed official source confirms that players can tailor their experience through a range of difficulty options. It does not say whether a selection can be changed after starting, where such a change would be made, whether it has restrictions, or whether different options create separate saves.

Those questions remain open. A future source may establish a switching policy, but a mode-selection interface should not be inferred from the existence of several named options.

## Do difficulty modes change rewards?

No reward difference is confirmed in the official material used here. S-GAME has not published difficulty-specific experience, materials, currency, loot, trophies, achievements, unlocks, or ending conditions for Wayfarer, Hellwalker, or Sixty-Six Days.

The absence of a published reward rule does not prove that no difference exists. It only means PBZ Guides cannot present one as a current fact.

## Difficulty in the wider combat system

Difficulty is one part of the game's published combat framework. The [Phantom Blade Zero combat system overview](/combat/combat-system/) covers the confirmed weapon loadout, upgrades, accessories, and other high-level information without treating pre-release descriptions as finalized frame data or balance rules.

For now, the useful distinction is the studio's own one: Wayfarer is aimed at story enjoyment, Hellwalker is a higher challenge with advanced and adaptive enemy intelligence, and Sixty-Six Days is a demanding option.
