## Quick answer

*Phantom Blade Zero* uses an action-combat framework built around two equipped main weapons and two equipped Phantom Edges. Official material describes a varied main arsenal, secondary weapons with less conventional approaches, accessories that can shape builds, weapon upgrades and reforging, and multiple difficulty options.

The game is still pre-release. S-GAME has outlined the system's equipment layers and player-expression goals, but it has not published a complete technical account of its launch-version combat rules, balance, or advanced mechanics.

## How does combat work in Phantom Blade Zero?

The confirmed model combines main weapons with Phantom Edges in a four-weapon loadout. S-GAME's official gameplay material describes an action system intended to give players different ways to approach combat: main weapons cover a broad arsenal, while Phantom Edges add less conventional options.

The available official descriptions focus on equipment choice and combat expression. They do not establish a stamina system, posture bar, parry timing, dodge invulnerability frames, combo multiplier, lock-on rule, cancellation window, or exact controller input. Those details should remain unresolved until S-GAME publishes them as mechanics.

## Main weapons

S-GAME currently confirms more than 30 main weapons. Its official material describes the main arsenal as including a wide variety of melee and ranged weapons, so the category is broader than one weapon type.

The reviewed sources do not publish a complete main-weapon list or a final classification for every weapon. They also do not identify exact damage values, weapon statistics, rarity levels, or best choices. The confirmed point is the scale and variety of the main arsenal, not a complete launch inventory.

## Phantom Edges

S-GAME currently describes 25 secondary weapons known as Phantom Edges. Two can be equipped alongside the two main weapons, and official material presents them as less conventional combat options that expand player expression.

For the category's confirmed details and its remaining unknowns, see [Phantom Edges in Phantom Blade Zero](/weapons/phantom-edges/). This overview does not treat Phantom Edges as spells, consumables, skills, or a complete public list of individual weapons.

## Loadout structure

The confirmed battle loadout is **two main weapons and two Phantom Edges**. This establishes the categories and number of equipped weapons, but not the complete behavior of changing them in combat.

S-GAME has not published the exact switching inputs, whether all four can be cycled immediately, or any universal cooldown or resource-cost rules. It would be inaccurate to infer those details from the number of equipped slots alone.

## Weapon upgrades and reforging

The 2026 State of Play deep dive says that weapons can be upgraded. It also says that players who reconsider an upgraded weapon or want to change a build can reforge it, recover the materials, and reinvest them in another weapon.

That is a confirmed broad system description. The reviewed sources do not name upgrade materials, list upgrade caps, define cost formulas, describe stat trees, or state that every weapon category follows identical rules. This page therefore keeps the upgrade and reforge explanation at the level S-GAME has published.

## Builds and accessories

S-GAME says weapons can be paired with different accessories to create the build a player wants. This confirms that accessories are part of the broader build-shaping system.

The official material reviewed here does not specify accessory-slot counts, individual bonuses, set effects, stat allocation, or optimal combinations. A build can be understood as an equipment choice within the published framework, not as a confirmed list of recommended end-game setups.

## Ranged and unusual combat options

The State of Play discussion says players have options to take down enemies from afar and to use summons for assistance in battle. These are examples of possibilities discussed around the broader weapon system.

They do not establish that every ranged option is a main weapon, that every summon is a Phantom Edge, or that either option has a shared mechanical rule. The safer conclusion is that the confirmed arsenal supports varied approaches, including range and assistance, while the exact division of features is not fully published.

## Difficulty options

S-GAME confirms multiple difficulty options. **Wayfarer** is positioned for players who would like to enjoy the story. **Hellwalker** is presented as a higher-challenge option with more advanced and adaptive enemy intelligence. **Sixty-Six Days** mode is also named as another demanding option.

The reviewed source does not publish numerical enemy-health changes, damage scaling, reward differences, trophies, or rules for changing difficulty. It also does not provide a full hierarchy beyond the descriptions above. Those details are not inferred here.

## What is not confirmed yet?

The official sources reviewed for this page do not provide a complete technical breakdown of parry timing, dodge invulnerability frames, exact combo rules, frame data, weapon-switch behavior, full build mathematics, or launch-version balance. They also do not support best-weapon rankings, optimal builds, or boss-specific tactics.

That leaves room for future official details, but it does not prevent a useful current overview: the published framework already confirms layered weapon choices, upgrades and material recovery through reforging, accessory-based build shaping, and multiple difficulty options.
